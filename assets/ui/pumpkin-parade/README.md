# Pumpkin Parade icons

Original generated transparent artwork for the user-approved first UI concept.

- Local source: `icons.png` (1774 x 887, RGBA).
- Roblox image: `rbxassetid://119439463451097`, uploaded through the connected Roblox Studio image upload tool.
- Layout: four columns, two rows. Row 1: Haunt, Mansion, Upgrades, Rebirth. Row 2: Collection, Vacuum, Light, Coin.
- `src/client/UITheme.luau` selects cells by clipping a proportionally sized ImageLabel. This tolerates Roblox texture resizing and preserves alpha.
- Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. The sheet was copied intact; no background removal or image processing was used.

## Generation prompt

Create a production-ready transparent PNG sprite atlas for Ghostlight Hollow, a kid-friendly Halloween Roblox game. Exactly 4 columns and 2 rows of equal square cells, overall 2:1 image. All 8 icons aligned in precise uniform cell grid, each centered in its own cell and occupies at most 72% width and 72% height of that cell, generous transparent gutters. No text, no labels, no numbers, no button backgrounds, no borders around cells, no gridlines, no cast shadows outside individual icons. Genuinely transparent background. Style matches the approved Pumpkin Parade mockup: premium cheerful toy-like 3D illustrated game icons, rich saturated pumpkin orange, violet, warm gold, cream and cyan, strong dark purple silhouette outline, highlights, readable at 64px. Row 1 left to right: (1) friendly smiling orange jack-o-lantern with curling green stem, (2) compact purple gothic haunted mansion with 3 pointed roofs and warm golden windows, (3) compact purple and teal ghost vacuum canister with a prominent green upward upgrade arrow, (4) two thick purple magical circular rebirth arrows with tiny gold sparkle. Row 2 left to right: (5) open purple and gold ghost collection book with a cute smiling white ghost emerging above the pages, (6) diagonal handheld purple vacuum nozzle with a glowing cyan round opening, (7) diagonal dark purple flashlight with warm golden lamp beam, (8) a single bright gold coin with dollar symbol embossed in center. Every item entirely contained within its cell, consistent scale and lighting, isolated transparent background. This is game icon art only, not a UI screen or mockup.
