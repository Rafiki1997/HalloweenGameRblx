# Round 7 visual critique

**FAIL.** The real Studio scene communicates the reference's broad idea and approximate district positions, but it is far from an exact visual match. Ignoring Studio chrome and infographic text, the reference is a compact, intricately terraced mountain town with crooked gothic architecture, stone paths, and localized pools of light. The current result reads as a mostly flat circular forest arena populated by repeated rectangular buildings and luminous road strips.

## Largest-impact fixes

1. **Rebuild the terrain and district footprint into stepped, tightly packed terraces.** The reference has a conspicuous cliff shelf below the mansion, raised individual haunt gardens, a substantial vertical outer rock face, and a town and graveyard filling the foreground. Here the mansion stair stands on a smooth slope, the outer edge is a thin uniform ring of rocks, and the lower third is largely conifer filler around an excessively long entry road. Elevate the mansion plateau and reveal a jagged retaining cliff across its front; give each showcase its own irregular stone shelf; move and expand the town and graveyard toward the entry; shorten the unoccupied foreground road. Preserve the lake on the left, but integrate its shoreline into those stepped rock shelves.

2. **Replace the repeated flat-front showcase compounds and open grids with crooked gothic building clusters.** All six prominent showcases currently reuse essentially the same broad flat facade, straight rectangular frontage, repeated luminous square windows, and rows of exposed paving pads. The reference compounds have irregular towers, projecting bays, porches, stair approaches, layered roofs, perimeter walls, and dense gardens. Remove the visible pad grids from this reference preview or replace them with dressed garden courts; break each silhouette into differently sized wings and spires, and bring dressed perimeter walls closer around them. Make the right-of-mansion showcase unmistakably green rather than its present cyan. The mansion also needs a more irregular, richly layered silhouette and a garden terrace between its doors and the main stair.

3. **Replace the uniformly bright cream road network with visibly jointed, muted stone streets and tightly controlled lighting.** Current paths are continuous broad yellow-white ribbons that dominate the map, while the reference uses mauve-gray cobbles with warm illumination concentrated around individual lanterns. Reduce path brightness and excessive warm light coverage, add legible irregular stone joints across the main route and plaza ring, and introduce darker intervals between lamps. Restore blue-violet ambient fill so roof and rock forms remain readable without turning the streets into light sources. Reduce the central ghost's blown-out glow and give it the reference's taller flowing silhouette over a more substantial stone fountain; the current bright round ghost overwhelms its basin.

## Improvement potential

The recognizable six-color showcase arrangement, left lake, lower-left shops, lower-right graveyard, central fountain, and rear mansion are a useful foundation. Large gains remain achievable through the three structural changes above; scattered prop additions alone will not close the gap. No earlier round was provided for this independent review, so a measured claim of improvement over previous rounds would be unsupported. Round 7 does not satisfy the requested exact reference appearance.
